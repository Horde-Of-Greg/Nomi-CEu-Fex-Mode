A fork of NFu with the goal of having people build cool big setups/bases through buffing lots of recipes and tweaking stuff I consider "too op" or dont like. Beware of opinionated changes!

Highlights include:

- Default to HM
- No Stab Miners 
- No energy p2p
- No rf -> eu conversion
- Buffed recipes for assorted stuff, increasingly so further along progression
